Clicks to a playing world

  1. Paired: T3 World → Create T3 World (2 clicks, or Enter twice).
  2. Unpaired: T3 World → Next: Connect agents → Paste link → Pair & create world (4), or Skip for now (3).
  3. Returning: T3 World → Continue (2).
  4. No confirmation dialogs anywhere. ✕ Plain world and Skip for now are one click and take effect immediately.
